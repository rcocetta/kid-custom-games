# Taekwondo moves — research for the pose author

Side view only, facing right (the fighter is mirrored when facing left).
"Back" = the rear leg/arm (far from the opponent). "Front" = the lead leg/arm (near the opponent).
Timings are rough estimates for a game feel, not measured values. Sources are at the bottom; items marked
**(est.)** are general knowledge, not from a source.

---

## 1. Sparring stance (fighting stance) and guard

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool stance page](https://taekwondopreschool.com/tutorialstance1.html), [Wikipedia: Taekwondo stances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taekwondo_stances), [Taekwondo Preschool fighting stance](https://taekwondopreschool.com/stances.html)

- **Feet:** about two shoulder-widths apart, front-to-back staggered (lead foot forward, toes toward the
  opponent). Feet roughly parallel to the ground; weight on the balls of the feet.
- **Legs:** both knees slightly bent (not locked). Weight shifted slightly onto the **back** leg so the
  front leg can lift quickly.
- **Torso:** turned about 30-45 degrees to the side (in profile it reads as a narrow silhouette), upright
  or leaning very slightly forward. Chin tucked.
- **Guard:**
  - Lead (front) fist up at about eye/temple height, in front of the face, forearm angled in, elbow down.
  - Rear fist up at the chin / cheek level, elbow tucked in, protecting the chest.
  - Elbows bent to about 90 degrees (both), forearms near vertical or slightly angled in.
- **Rear arm and front arm are both visible in side view** (the spec asks for this): the front fist is
  forward, the rear fist is beside the chin.
- **Idle bounce (est.):** the whole body rises and falls about 2-4 % of standing height, 1 cycle every
  ~0.6-0.8 s, with the knees flexing a few degrees more at the bottom. Do not move the guard hands.

Joint notes for the author (est.): knees ~150-160 degrees (interior), hips slightly bent, torso ~10-15
degrees forward from vertical in profile, elbows ~90 degrees, fists at head height (front) and chin (rear).

## 2. Walking forward and back in stance

Source: [Taekwondo Preschool stance / movement](https://taekwondopreschool.com/tutorialstance1.html) (stance
basics only; movement mechanics are general knowledge (est.)).

- **Step forward:** the rear foot slides or steps past the front foot toward the target, keeping the same
  staggered width; the front leg leads, the rear follows. Body stays in the same guard. Medium speed:
  ~0.25-0.3 s for one step.
- **Step back:** the front foot moves back first, then the rear foot follows to re-establish the stance.
  Keep the guard up. Medium speed, ~0.25-0.3 s.
- **Shuffle (est.):** short, fast alternate slide with feet barely leaving the ground. Use for the
  "advance / retreat" feel without big leg swings.
- Knees stay bent through the step; the body does not bob much; the hands stay up.

## 3. Jump and duck

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool jumping spin hook page](https://taekwondopreschool.com/jumping_spinhook_kick.html)
(jump context only); general knowledge for the rest (est.).

- **Jump (est.):** from the stance, a small knee bend (crouch, ~120-130 degrees knee), then a short push.
  In the air the legs tuck slightly, the back leg may trail, the guard stays up. Landing back in stance.
  Total air time ~0.5-0.6 s. Jump height ~15-20 % of body height.
- **Duck (crouch) (est.):** from stance, both knees bend deeply (~90 degrees or less), the hips drop and the
  torso leans forward ~20-30 degrees, the rear hand drops to near the chin of the rear side, the front
  hand stays near the head. Crouch takes ~0.15-0.2 s down, same to come back up.
- In the game: duck = head-level attacks miss; jump = low attacks miss.

## 4. Punches

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool strike tutorial](https://taekwondopreschool.com/tutorial_strike2.html),
[NBTKDA punches](https://www.nbtkda.com/punches), [tkdtutor: fore-fist / straight punch](https://tkdtutor.blogspot.com/p/martial-arts-taekwondo-fore-fist.html),
[tkdtutor: hip snap vs winding vs sine wave](https://tkdtutor.blogspot.com/p/martial-arts-taekwondo-snap-winding-wave.html)

### 4a. Front-hand jab (*jaeb jireugi*) — game key S — MID (body)
- Names: *jaeb jireugi* (jab); also "front fist".
- Start: stance; front fist at head height, elbow down.
- Extension: front arm straight from the shoulder, fist travels straight forward. Fist rotates ~90 degrees
  so the knuckles face forward at impact. Small torso lean forward (~5-10 degrees) and slight hip
  rotation.
- Target height: body (the spec's MID).
- Front leg stays put; rear leg stays put or just bends a little. No step.
- Retract: fist back to guard in the same line.
- Timing: fast — ~0.12 s out, ~0.12 s back.
- Reach: shortest (front arm, from the front side). Short-range.

### 4b. Back-hand straight punch (*ap jireugi* / rear straight) — game key A — MID (body)
- Names: *ap jireugi* (straight punch, rear hand); the source calls this the "rear fist / rear-hand straight".
- Start: stance; rear fist at chin.
- Chamber: the rear fist pulls back slightly toward the cheek, elbow down; hip starts to turn.
- Extension: rear arm drives straight out, hips rotate forward (rear hip comes in), rear heel may lift, the
  body shifts forward into the punch. Rear fist rotates to knuckles-up/forward at impact.
- Target height: body (MID). Head punches are NOT legal in WT sparring (see Section 10).
- Retract: back to chin.
- Timing: medium — ~0.15-0.18 s out, similar back.
- Reach: longer than the jab (full arm + body shift).

### 4c. Low punch (down + attack) — LOW
- Same shape as the jab (front) or straight (rear), but the fist goes **down to hip / upper thigh** height.
  Knees bend a little more (~140 degrees), torso lean forward ~10-15 degrees. Use for game LOW level.
- Source-based note: low attacks are not legal in WT sparring; this is a self-defense / game version (est.).

### 4d. Jumping punch (up + attack) — HIGH
- Real in the game only. Start with a small jump (see Section 3), then extend the front or rear fist
  straight out at body / head height, legs tucked. Land back in stance. Keep the same levels as the base
  punch but the body is in the air (not hit by LOW).

### 4e. Spinning punch — see Section 6 (no real TKD spinning punch; closest is the spinning backfist).

## 5. Kicks

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool roundhouse](https://taekwondopreschool.com/roundhouse.html),
[Taekwondo Preschool front-foot roundhouse](https://taekwondopreschool.com/front_foot_roundhouse_kick.html),
[Taekwondo Preschool spin hook kick](https://www.taekwondopreschool.com/spinhookkick.html),
[Taekwondo Wiki: front kick](https://taekwondo.fandom.com/wiki/Front_Kick),
[Wikipedia: Roundhouse kick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roundhouse_kick),
[Blackbelt wiki: spinning back kick](https://blackbeltwiki.com/?p=4044)

### 5a. Front-leg kick — game key X — MID
Real name chosen: **front-leg roundhouse (*ap dollyeo chagi* / "apbal dollyeo chagi")**. It is the most
typical front-leg kick in WT sparring and the closest to what the game wants. "Cut kick" is not a standard
TKD term in these sources, so it is not used here.
- Stance: starts from the fighting stance, lead leg lifts.
- Chamber: lead knee drawn up and across, shin pointing down-forward, knee ~45-60 degrees bent.
- Pivot: the **rear** (standing) foot pivots on the ball, hips turn toward the target (small rotation, the
  front leg needs less hip turn than the rear version).
- Extension: lower leg whips horizontally across, striking with the **instep** (*baldeung*) at chest/abdomen
  (MID) or head (HIGH). Source: front leg is quicker but weaker than the rear roundhouse.
- Arms: lead fist stays up near the face; rear fist stays at the chin (cover). The kicking leg's side
  arm may counter-balance slightly.
- Torso: leans back a little (~5-10 degrees) to keep balance.
- Retract: leg returns to the stance, same chamber line.
- Timing: fast — ~0.12 s out, ~0.1 s back.
- Reach: medium-short (front leg covers less distance).

### 5b. Rear-leg roundhouse (*dollyo chagi* / *dollyeo chagi*) — game key Z/W — HIGH (head kick)
This is the "back kick" of the spec and the most powerful kick.
- Stance: standing on the **front** leg, rear leg lifts.
- Chamber: rear knee drawn up high and across the body, thigh horizontal, shin hanging down (~60-90
  degrees knee bend). The hip turns with the knee.
- Pivot: front (standing) foot pivots about 135-180 degrees (heel turns away from the target, toes point
  to the opponent's side). WT school standards cite 135-180 degrees; the pivot is one school's standard.
- Extension: knee rotates so the shin is nearly horizontal, then snaps straight out. The hip is fully
  rotated, the body leans slightly away (~10-15 degrees) and the standing leg is bent (~150 degrees).
- Strike: **instep** (ball of foot is an alternative), at chest/abdomen (MID) or head (HIGH, not in the
  game's MID rule).
- Arms: the **rear** fist drops into the body a little for balance; the **front** fist stays up as guard.
- Retract: the leg chambers back and returns to the stance.
- Timing: medium — ~0.2-0.25 s out (the hip turn takes longer), ~0.2 s back.
- Reach: longest of the basic kicks.

### 5c. Low kick (down + kick) — LOW
- Same as 5a or 5b, but the foot goes to **shin / knee height** (not the head). Shin/knee-strike height
  is not in WT sparring (below-waist strikes are illegal in sparring; WT sources list knees/thighs as
  self-defense only). Use it for the game's LOW level.
- Chamber low: knee lower, the hip doesn't lift as much; the kick is more horizontal near the floor.
- Standing leg bent more (~140 degrees).

### 5d. Jumping kick (up + kick) — HIGH
- Real name: *ttwieo* (jumping) + the kick (e.g. jumping front kick, or *ttwieo dollyeo chagi* — jumping
  turn kick, a real TKD technique, in the Blackbelt wiki list). Use the **roundhouse-style** jump kick for
  the game: the body leaves the ground, the chamber is the same as 5a/5b, the kicking leg extends at head
  height.
- Air: body tilted ~10-20 degrees forward, standing leg tucks, kicking leg straight (~160 degrees).
- Landing: back on the same foot, return to stance.
- Timing: fast in the air, total ~0.5 s for the whole jump.
- Reach: good at head height.

## 6. Spinning moves

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool: spin hook kick](https://www.taekwondopreschool.com/spinhookkick.html),
[Taekwondo Preschool: jumping spin hook kick](https://taekwondopreschool.com/jumping_spinhook_kick.html),
[ojs.ub.uni-konstanz: spin hook kick study](https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/cpa/article/view/6101/5581),
[Blackbelt wiki: spinning back kick](https://blackbeltwiki.com/?p=4044),
[Blackbelt wiki: flying back kick](https://blackbeltwiki.com/?p=3965)

### 6a. Spin hook kick / back spin whip (*dwi huryeo chagi*) — game: back + front limb, HIGH
- Also called: heel kick, reverse turning kick, spinning hook kick, wheel kick.
- Stance: fighting stance. Turn the body away from the target; the **rear** leg is the usual kicking leg
  (the heel is the strike surface in traditional sparring; the ball/sole in WT).
- Pivot: the front (standing) foot pivots and the body turns about 180 degrees (the most common WT form is
  a 180-degree hook; full 360/540 are demonstration forms).
- Chamber: the rear leg comes around behind the body, leg straight or lightly bent, at waist height.
- Extension: the leg whips around in a horizontal arc, a hook/whip toward the head (jaw/temple, HIGH). The
  body's back faces the opponent for part of the motion.
- Arms: the **front** fist stays up near the face, the rear arm swings slightly with the turn.
- Head: eyes look over the shoulder at the target.
- Standing leg: knee bent (~150 degrees) for balance.
- Timing: slow to start, medium overall — ~0.3-0.35 s out (windup visible), ~0.25 s back.
- Reach: long (the arc).
- **Plan check:** the spec says the *front* limb is used for the head-height spin. The real spin hook kick
  is normally done with the *rear* leg. A front-leg spinning hook exists in some forms, but it is less
  standard. Consider mapping "back + front" to a front-leg spin (or keep the name and note the difference).

### 6b. Jumping spin hook kick (*ttwieo dwi huryeo chagi*, "jumping back whip kick") — back + Up + kick, HIGH
- Real move. Same arc as 6a but the body jumps first. Eyes and head look over the shoulder.
- Timing: total ~0.6 s with the jump.

### 6c. Spinning back kick (*dwit chagi* / "back kick") — back + back limb, MID
- Real move name in the game: "spinning back kick" (*dwit chagi* in the sources; the spec uses "spinning back
  kick").
- Stance: fighting stance. Step forward or pivot with the front foot; turn the body **away** from the
  target (the back of the body faces the opponent for a moment).
- Chamber: the **rear** leg is pulled through toward the target by the hip turn; the knee is bent.
- Extension: the leg is driven straight back (a straight line, the body is spinning), heel first, at
  waist/body (MID). Sources note it is a straight-line kick, not a full spin.
- Arms: the front arm stays as guard; the rear arm swings in the turn.
- Body: almost 360-degree turn in some forms; the final orientation is facing the opponent again.
- Head: over the shoulder.
- Timing: slow windup, medium-fast strike — ~0.3 s out, ~0.25 s back.
- Reach: medium.
- Risk (source): with the leg held far from the body and back turned, the opponent gets a free attack
  until the fighter turns back.

### 6d. Spinning backfist — game "back + front punch" (HIGH)
- **Not a standard TKD technique** (no TKD name in the sources). The closest real move is the *spinning
  back fist* from general karate/martial-arts (an arc of the front arm with the back of the fist).
- Use as a game move: the body turns 180 degrees, the front arm swings in a horizontal arc at head height,
  striking with the back of the fist. Slower than a punch. ~0.3 s out.

### 6e. Spinning back punch (back + back limb, MID)
- **Not a real TKD technique.** Closest real move: the spinning back fist with the rear arm, or a spinning
  reverse punch (a karate-style technique). Use as a game move and call it "spinning back fist (rear arm)".

### 6f. Low spinning sweep (back + down + kick) — LOW
- **No standard TKD name found in the sources.** The closest real moves: a spinning low kick (the
  spinning back kick at ankle/knee height) or a sweep kick. The spin hook at low height is a reasonable
  game version: 6a with the foot at knee / shin height, the body low (knee ~130 degrees).
- Timing: slow, ~0.35 s out.

### 6g. Jumping spinning kick (back + up + kick) — HIGH
- Use 6b (jumping back whip kick). Real name: *ttwieo dwi huryeo chagi*.

## 7. Blocks (*makki*)

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool blocks page](https://taekwondopreschool.com/blocks.html),
[Taekwondo Preschool: low/middle/high block tutorials](https://taekwondopreschool.com/tutorialblock5.html),
[NBTKDA blocks](https://www.nbtkda.com/blocks),
[Wikipedia: Blocking technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blocking_technique)

Note: the sources disagree on exact hand heights (especially the middle block). The values below are the
common version; the author can adjust.

### 7a. High block (*olgul makki*) — standing block, covers HIGH + MID
- Start: the blocking arm across the abdomen / waist, fist up, elbow bent.
- Action: the forearm rises in an arc, the wrist ends one fist-width in front of the forehead,
  elbow out, palm facing outward. The other hand pulls to the side of the waist (fist at the hip).
- Torso: upright, slight lean back to stay out of range.
- Timing: medium-fast, ~0.15 s to block position.

### 7b. Middle block (*momtong makki*) — standing block, covers MID (and HIGH in the game)
- Start: the blocking arm across the abdomen; or the guard position.
- Action: the forearm rises to chest / shoulder height, elbow about 90 degrees, forearm roughly
  across the body, the chest stays open. The pulling hand stays at the side of the waist.
- Timing: medium-fast, ~0.15 s.

### 7c. Low block (*arae makki*) — ducking block, covers LOW + MID
- Start: the blocking arm low, fist across the abdomen / hip, palm up or down.
- Action: the forearm lifts to shoulder height then snaps down to the outside of the leg (thigh), palm
  down, elbow bent at the end. The other hand pulls back to the waist.
- The body: knees bend more (~130 degrees), the torso leans forward a little.
- Timing: fast, ~0.15 s.

## 8. Hit reaction, bow, win and defeat

Sources: [Taekwondo Preschool bow page](https://taekwondopreschool.com/bow.html),
[Wikipedia: Taekwondo stances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taekwondo_stances),
[ITF encyclopedia (kyungye, tenets)](https://romashikk.github.io/Encyclopedia-of-Taekwon-Do/vol02.html)

### 8a. Hit reaction (game only, est.)
- Head hit: head snaps back ~15 degrees, the torso leans back ~10 degrees, the arms come up loosely,
  eyes squeezed. ~0.25 s.
- Body hit: the torso folds forward ~10 degrees, the arms drop slightly, ~0.25 s.
- Legs hit: the knees bend, the body dips ~5 degrees, ~0.2 s.
- Block: the blocking arm stays in position, the body shifts back a little.

### 8b. Bow (*kyungye*)
- Source: the bow command is *kyungye*. The bow is from the attention position (arms at the sides, feet
  together). In the game: stand, arms straight at the sides, then the torso bends about 30 degrees from
  the hips, head follows, hold ~0.5 s, return. Good for the start/end of a fight.
- Source note: the ready position (*joonbi*) varies by school (fists just below the belly button, or closed
  fists in front of the waist); the bow starts from attention.

### 8c. Winning pose (est.)
- Feet apart, weight even, one fist raised above the head (lead arm), the other fist at the hip. Chest
  open, head up, a small hop or a front kick raised slightly. Hold ~1-2 s.

### 8d. Defeated (KO-style, kid-friendly) pose (est.)
- Sitting on the floor with the legs out, hands on the floor behind for support, head tilted, eyes closed
  or dizzy. Little stars or circles around the head (dazed). No injury, no blood.
- Use a short slump: torso falls back, arms relax.

## 10. Where the game's simplified rules differ from WT sparring

Source for WT rules: [Taekwondo Preschool roundhouse page](https://taekwondopreschool.com/roundhouse.html) (targets)
and general WT knowledge (est.).

- **Punches to the head:** not legal in WT Olympic sparring (only kicks to head, punches to the trunk).
  The game (plan section 2) has HIGH punches (jumping punch, spinning backfist) — a game-only rule.
- **Low attacks (below the waist, knee/shin):** not legal in WT sparring; sources list knees/thighs as
  self-defence only. The game's LOW level is a game-only rule.
- **Spin hook kick with the front limb:** the real spin hook is usually rear-leg. See 6a.
- **Pivot angles and chamber:** the sources give 135-180 degrees for the rear roundhouse, but schools differ.
- **Spin kick rotation:** WT competition often uses 180-degree hook kicks rather than full spins.
