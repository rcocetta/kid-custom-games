# Whack-a-m-Oli
We are describing here a new game to build. Like the other games in this folder, we want to stick with an HTML, front-end only game. No data tracking or transfer, beyond the basic files used for it.

## The game
The background and design style should be the same as the one in /jumpnduck. 
The main character is Oli, same design. 

He stands at the top of the screen on a suspended platform, a gondola. 

He can throw hammers (I'll later define instructions as to how)

At the bottom of the screen we have some grass, and from it some moles come out, with speed and number that increases as you move forward. 

Food (carrot, broccoli, steak) appears on random positions in the screen, far but reachable to the moles. One at a time, and then 2 at higher levels.

The moles start moving in the opposite direction of a vegetable (they're blind), but when they touch the end of the screen they come back, to go to eat it. 

Oli needs to throw hammers (rotating hammers) to the moles from above, to prevent them from eating the food. 

## The rules
Each mole gives him 100 points. 
If moles eat 5 of his food, he loses. 

The moles come out starting from 1 every 3 seconds, and moving slowly, and then they gradually appear faster (after 1000 points the level switches), and move faster.

## The controls
### desktop
Space to launch
Left/Right arrow to move. 

### mobile
Tap to launch
Move finger left/right to move.
